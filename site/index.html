DBTC — frozen-law tracker

DBTC = P0 × (Ds/D0)b — the difficulty→price law was frozen on and is never re-fitted. Only network difficulty and FX move; the law does not. Charts: drag/wheel to zoom, click any legend item to show or hide it.
